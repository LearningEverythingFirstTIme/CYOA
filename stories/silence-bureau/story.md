# start

You stand on the observation deck of Level 847, watching the lower districts drown in a symphony of poverty. Up here, in the Whisper District, silence is so thick you could cut it with a blade.

Your earpiece crackles. A client. Anonymous, of course—they always are. But this one is different. They don't want to buy silence. They want you to find something that shouldn't exist: the Lethal Frequency. A sound that kills. A note that ends lives.

The ultimate silence, rendered in the most violent frequency imaginable. The message contains coordinates. Three possible sources. Each leads to a different world within this city of sonic commerce.
