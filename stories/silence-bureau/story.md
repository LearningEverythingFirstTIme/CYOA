# start
**Location:** THE WHISPER DISTRICT

The rain in Audivale doesn't fall—it *sings*. Each droplet carries a microtone of value, a crystalline note that patters against the graphene canyons of the vertical city. You stand on the observation deck of Level 847, watching the lower districts drown in a symphony of poverty. Up here, in the Whisper District, silence is so thick you could cut it with a blade.

Your earpiece crackles. A client. Anonymous, of course—they always are. But this one is different. They don't want to buy silence. They want you to find something that shouldn't exist: the Lethal Frequency. A sound that kills. A note that ends lives.

The ultimate silence, rendered in the most violent frequency imaginable. The message contains coordinates. Three possible sources. Each leads to a different world within this city of sonic commerce.

# archivist_start
**Location:** THE ECHO ARCHIVES

The Archives exist in a state of temporal contradiction—simultaneously ancient and impossibly new. You descend through seventeen security checkpoints, each one stripping away more of your personal sound signature. By the time you reach the main vault, your own footsteps make no noise. You have become a ghost in a cathedral of preserved audio.

The space defies description. Sound doesn't echo here; it *remembers*. The walls are lined with crystalline storage matrices, each one containing centuries of human audio history. A baby's first cry from 2041. The last words of a dying president. The ambient hum of cities that no longer exist.

An archivist approaches. Her robes are woven with fiber-optic threads that pulse with the rhythm of stored audio. She recognizes you—not your face, but your *silence*. Your reputation precedes you like a held breath.

"You're here for the Frequency," she says. Not a question. "But do you want to find it... or bury it?"

- [Admit you want to find it—for a client, for profit → archivist_profit]
- [Claim you want to ensure it's never found → archivist_bury]
- [Ask what she knows about its origins first → archivist_origins]

# archivist_profit
**Location:** THE ECHO ARCHIVES

"The Frequency was created during the Sonic Wars," the archivist says, leading you deeper into the Archives. "Not as a weapon. As a *mistake*. A resonance experiment gone wrong. The scientists who discovered it didn't die immediately—they recorded everything. Their own deaths, captured in audio. The ultimate documentation."

She stops before a sealed chamber marked with a symbol you don't recognize: three concentric circles with a vertical line through the center. The Door of Forbidden Echoes.

"Those recordings exist. Somewhere. But they're not here. The Archives were raided six months ago. Someone stole the original documentation. All we have left are fragments—references, citations, theoretical frameworks."

She hands you a data crystal. It pulses with a cold blue light.

"This contains everything we know about the theft. The trail leads to the Memory Merchants in the Resonance Market. But be warned: they don't sell memories to just anyone. They require... payment in kind."

# archivist_bury
**Location:** THE HALL OF FORGOTTEN THINGS

When you speak of burying the Frequency, the archivist's fiber-optic robes brighten—pulsing with warm amber tones. Recognition. Kinship, even.

"Few come to the Archives with such intent," she says, her voice dropping to a frequency that resonates in your chest. "Most seek power. Control. The ability to silence their enemies permanently. But you understand—the Frequency is an abomination against the natural order of sound."

She leads you to a section of the Archives you've never seen before: the Hall of Forgotten Things. Here, recordings aren't preserved—they're *contained*. Sealed in null-field chambers where time itself seems muted. The air tastes of absence.

"We are the Keepers of What Must Not Be Remembered," she explains. "A secret order within the Archives. We believe some sounds deserve oblivion. The Frequency is our highest priority. But we cannot find it alone. The trail grows cold in the Resonance Market, where memory is currency and truth is negotiable."

She presses a small device into your palm. A resonance tracker, calibrated to detect the unique signature of the original Frequency recordings.

"Work with us. Help us ensure the Lethal Frequency is destroyed, not sold. And in exchange, we will show you secrets the Archives have guarded for centuries."

- [Accept her offer—join the Keepers → ending_harmonic]
- [Pretend to accept, but plan to betray them → ending_absolute]
- [Ask what other secrets they guard → ending_capital]

# archivist_origins
**Location:** THE ARCHIVES - ORIGINS WING

Your question hangs in the air, and for a moment, the Archives themselves seem to listen. The archivist tilts her head—a gesture of evaluation—and the fiber-optic threads in her robes shift to a contemplative purple.

"You ask the right questions," she says. "Most rush to the answer without understanding the question. The Lethal Frequency was born in Laboratory 7, during the final days of the Sonic Wars. The researchers were trying to create the perfect silence—a frequency that would cancel all other sound. Instead, they created something that cancels... life."

She leads you to a holographic display. It shows schematics, equations, fragments of recovered documentation. The science is beyond you—quantum acoustics, bio-resonance theory, something called "cognitive frequency harmonics."

"The Frequency doesn't just kill. It *records* its victims. Every person who dies hearing it becomes part of its waveform. It grows. Evolves. The original researchers—twenty-three of them—are still in there. Screaming. Forever."

The display flickers, and for a split second, you see something that shouldn't exist: a visual representation of the Frequency itself. It looks like a wound in reality. A tear where sound becomes anti-sound.

- [Ask why she trusts you with this information → ending_harmonic]

# debt_start
**Location:** THE SOUND OF EMPTY

You wake to the hollow ring of your own silence debt. The apartment's acoustic dampeners hum their low warning tone—three days until cutoff. Your name is whispered across the debt registry, a digital echo that follows you through the vertical sprawl of Audivale.

The city breathes around you. Forty million souls, each paying for every decibel they produce. The poor scream into sound-capture booths, trading their voices for credits. The wealthy purchase entire orchestras of manufactured quiet, living in acoustic bubbles of perfect, purchased peace.

You are a Silence Broker. You deal in stolen quiet and suppressed secrets. But tonight, a new opportunity has surfaced—a client willing to pay in pure, unregistered silence for information about the Lethal Frequency. A sound that kills anyone who hears it. A weapon. A myth. A payday.

Your client waits in the Lower Tiers. But first, you need to decide how to approach this.

# child_start
**Location:** THE HUSH CHILD

You kneel to the child's level—standard technique for interviewing juvenile witnesses, though this feels different. The child doesn't flinch when you meet their eyes. Instead, they extend the audiograph cylinder with both hands, an offering or a burden.

The cylinder is warm, as if recently played. No label, no markings, just the faint scent of ozone that suggests recent recording. When you turn it over, you find a single word scratched into the wax: *LISTEN*.

"Do you know what's on this?" you sign, using the trade-standard hand-talk common among muted debtors.

The child signs back with surprising fluency: *You told me not to say.*

Before you can process this, the Debt Collector clears his throat—an artificial sound, generated by his vocalizer. "Thirty seconds, Broker. The inheritance won't recover itself."

The Archivist watches with academic detachment. The child—small, silent, suddenly the most important thing in the room—waits for your decision.

- [Take the child to your soundproof apartment → ending_harmonic]
- [Go directly to the Hush District → ending_absolute]
- [Play the audiograph immediately, here in the Bureau → ending_white_noise]

# ending_white_noise {ending}
**Location:** ENDING: WHITE NOISE
**Theme:** Destruction can be creation. The old must die for the new to grow.

You press the broadcast button. The Frequency doesn't scream—it *sings*. A note so pure, so absolute, that it erases everything it touches.

Across Audivale, the Sound Repositories crumble. The acoustic taxation systems fail. The purchased experiences dissolve into genuine silence. Those who survive are changed. They remember what it was like to hear their own thoughts. To experience emotion without engineered accompaniment. To exist in spaces that aren't constantly selling them something.

The city learns to value silence again. Not as absence, but as potential.

You do not live to see it. The Frequency takes you too—something in your resonance makes you susceptible. As the city awakens to true silence, you fade into it. A broker who gave everything for the quietest revolution.

# ending_static {ending}
**Location:** ENDING: STATIC
**Theme:** Safety is its own prison. You chose preservation over transformation.

You hand the resonator to those who would control it. Their fingers close around it with the tenderness of lovers, the calculation of bankers. The Frequency will be studied, contained, weaponized in secret.

The auditory economy will continue, now with an ultimate threat held in reserve against those who might challenge it.

You are rewarded. Wealth beyond your previous imagination. A penthouse in the upper levels where the air is clean and the sounds are curated by personal acoustic engineers. You have everything you ever brokered for.

And every night, you hear it—the Frequency, or your imagination of it, playing in the spaces between your thoughts. Reminding you that you had the power to change everything and chose instead to preserve your own comfort.

The city continues. The economy continues. The prison continues. And you are its most comfortable inmate.

# ending_harmonic {ending}
**Location:** ENDING: HARMONIC RESOLUTION
**Theme:** Revolution doesn't require annihilation. Transformation can be gentle.

With guidance from those who understand what the Frequency truly is, you reshape it. It is not easy. It is not painless. The process requires sacrifice—your hearing, ultimately, burned away by the sonic fires of transformation.

But when it is done, the Frequency is something new. You broadcast it across the city. And instead of death, it brings *awakening*.

The auditory economy doesn't collapse—it evolves. People hear the manipulation in the sounds they purchase. They recognize the prison of engineered emotion. They begin to choose differently.

The change is slow. Imperfect. Filled with setbacks and resistance. But it is real. The city learns to value authentic sound over manufactured experience.

You live in silence now, but it is not empty. It is full of the memory of sound, the potential of change, the knowledge that you helped create something better without destroying what came before.

# ending_absolute {ending}
**Location:** ENDING: ABSOLUTE ZERO
**Theme:** Some prices are too high. You chose transformation so complete it became annihilation.

You turn the Frequency inward. It doesn't resist. It has been waiting for this—for someone willing to become the silence rather than merely wield it.

The transformation is instant and eternal. You cease to exist as a person and become a phenomenon. A walking absence. A broker who deals only in endings.

Where you walk, sound dies. Music fades. Voices fail. The auditory economy cannot function in your presence. You become a weapon of mass destruction and a symbol of ultimate liberation.

Some worship you. Others fear you. Debates rage about whether you are angel or demon.

But you are none of these things. You are silence itself. No thought, no memory, no self. Just the endless quiet that existed before creation and will persist after its end.

# ending_capital {ending}
**Location:** ENDING: CAPITAL OF SILENCE
**Theme:** The system corrupts everything it touches. You became its perfect expression.

You don't use the Frequency. You *sell* it.

An auction unlike any in history. Bidders from every faction, every level, every secret society. The Church offers faith. The revolutionaries offer purpose. The government offers power. Private collectors offer wealth beyond calculation.

You take all of it. Payment from everyone, delivery to no one. You broker the ultimate deal—the possibility of destruction, held in escrow, available to whoever meets your price.

The city enters a new era. Not revolution, not preservation, not transformation. Speculation. The Frequency becomes currency more valuable than sound itself. Futures markets emerge. Derivatives. Insurance policies against sonic annihilation.

You sit at the center of it all, the broker who controls the end of the world. Rich beyond measure. Powerful beyond challenge. And utterly, completely alone.

Eventually, someone will steal it from you. Eventually, someone will use it. Eventually, the silence you brokered will come for you too.

But not today. Today, you are king of the quiet. Emperor of the echo. The greatest Silence Broker who ever lived.
